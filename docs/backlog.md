# Future ideas

These are proposals, not authorized implementation work.

## FR-001 — Harvest plants or grow bounce spots

Allow planted potatoes to be harvested after a delay, or grow into mushrooms/other bounce spots. Potential choice: harvest now or help a later flight. Define rewards, real-time versus round-based growth, planting-location persistence and extra-object cap first. Short rounds must remain playable without waiting.

## FR-002 — In-flight collection sprouts

Small sprouts or a short collection beam could collect nearby loot. Define aiming, charges and separation from touch boost controls. Test a playable prototype before committing to a system; keep rounds short and controls simple.

## SPEC-006 — MiniZap announcement channel

Prefer a shared Telegram broadcast channel for games/releases, with optional linked discussion. Keep actionable requests in project tickets. URL, ownership and moderation are not yet defined. See [contact convention](specifications/minizap-about.md).
